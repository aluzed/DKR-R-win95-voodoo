# CRT filter image notice

DKR-R includes six optional PNG display masks under `assets/filters`.

- `scanlines-soft.png`, `shadow-mask.png` and `aperture-grille.png` were
  supplied from the project owner's DK64 Recompiled filter set.
- `Perfect_CRT-240p-BRT.png`, `Perfect_CRT-240p.png` and `Perfect_CRT.png`
  were separately supplied by the project owner for DKR-R.

The images are optional display data and are not extracted from Diddy Kong
Racing. Their exact SHA-256 values are recorded in `assets/filters/README.md`
and the release source tree. A public distributor remains responsible for
confirming redistribution rights for supplied artwork.
