# RDP → Glide combiner mapping

Generated by `tools/win95/gen_combiner_table.py`. The definitions are taken from
`include/PR/gbi.h` and `include/f3ddkr.h`; the list of configurations comes from
`combiner-inventory.md`, itself generated from the game's static tables.

Classification **measured on the machine** on 14 August 2026, not deduced.

## The structural correspondence

Glide has `GR_COMBINE_FUNCTION_SCALE_OTHER_MINUS_LOCAL_ADD_LOCAL`, which computes
`f × (other − local) + local`. That is **exactly** the RDP's form, `(a − b) × c +
d`, as soon as `d = b`. Nearly every one of DKR's configurations satisfies that
equality — both pieces of hardware express the same intent: interpolate between
two colours.

## What the card said, and what memory had wrong

The combiner's enumeration values were the only ones in the project never to have
been measured. `glide_state_probe.c` had checked blending, depth, scissoring and
the alpha test; not these. The harness caught them out with 140 to 156 units of
deviation across the whole `BLENDI`/`BLENDT` family.

`combine_enum_probe.c`'s sweep established, value by value:

| What is measured | Value | Observed behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| function | 1 | `LOCAL` — returns the local colour |
| function | 3 | `SCALE_OTHER` — returns `f × other` |
| function | 4 | `SCALE_OTHER_ADD_LOCAL` |
| function | 7 | `f × (other − local) + local` |
| factor | 1 | the local colour |
| factor | 8 | one |
| factor | 9 | `1 − local` (a **colour**, not a scalar) |

And the negative result, which is the most important: **none of the sixteen factor
values delivers the constant register's alpha**. The confirmed factors are all
functions of the local colour, or constants.

That reclassifies the whole family resting on `ENV_ALPHA` as a factor. It was
declared exact on the strength of values written from memory; it is now
approximate, and will stay so until a way out has been measured.

A way out exists and has not been tried: `ENV_ALPHA` is a constant known to the
processor, hence carriable in the vertex alpha, where `LOCAL_ALPHA` would go and
fetch it. Until that is measured, the classification stays cautious — announcing as
exact what is not is precisely the defect this ticket warns against.

> **And E05-S06 has since restricted it.** The fog factor already occupies the
> vertex alpha, on the 74 `G_RM_FOG_SHADE_A` render modes — the game's most
> frequent. The way out is therefore not general: it holds only for the
> configurations without fog, which makes it conditional rather than impossible.
> See `win95-fog.md`.

## The combiner truncates

A constant of 32 comes back as 28, one of 96 as 90: the combiner multiplies in
0..255 by 255/256 and truncates, which costs one quantisation step. That is not a
translation error but the hardware, and the harness models it — without which every
configuration would carry a systematic deviation of eight or nine units that would
drown the real ones.

## The two walls

**A single constant colour register.** The RDP has `PRIMITIVE` and `ENVIRONMENT`;
Glide has only `grConstantColorValue`.

It must not be declared too early. Glide has **separate** colour and alpha
combiners, and the vertex alpha belongs to us. If the colour term reads only one
register and the alpha term the other, the second travels in the vertex: both are
constant per draw call, hence known to the processor at the moment of writing the
vertices. The price is named — the vertex alpha can no longer carry anything else —
so the manoeuvre applies only if the alpha term does not read `SHADE`.

**`TEXEL1`.** Three configurations read two texels: that is not an approximation
but a referral to E05-S04.

## The second cycle folds away more often than one thinks

`G_CC_PASS2` is `(0,0,0,COMBINED)`, that is the identity; a second stage of the
form `(COMBINED, 0, X, 0)` is a scaling that composes into a simple `SCALE_OTHER`.
Without that folding, every two-cycle configuration would be multipass, which would
double the fill on the game's most common surfaces — and fill rate is what limits a
Voodoo 2 at 640×480.

## An unknown configuration

Logged, then rendered through a fallback that is **visible but not aberrant**: a
texture modulated by the vertex colour, the inventory's most frequent behaviour.
The two opposite reflexes are ruled out — drawing nothing would make a piece of
scenery disappear without a trace, painting in an alert colour would make the game
unplayable at the first forgotten combiner.

## What remains open

- The way out through the vertex alpha for the `ENV_ALPHA` family, unmeasured.
- The fill cost of multipass, unmeasured: it requires a representative scene, hence
  the ROM.
- The three two-texel configurations, which belong to E05-S04.

**Every configuration declared exact is exact**, to within one quantisation step.
It is the only check the harness counts as a failure: the `multipass` and
`approximate` categories announce a deviation, and measuring it is their reason for
being.

The residual eight units on the three `DECAL*` come from the texture path, where
the texel crosses the combiner with a factor of one — hence the same 255/256
truncation, which the model does not apply to the texel. Systematic, benign, and
reported rather than hidden by a widened tolerance.

The deviations of 99 to 148 are the ones single-pass translation cannot avoid
today. **No acceptance threshold is set yet**: the way out through the vertex alpha
has not been tried, and accepting a deviation one knows may be avoidable would be
premature.
